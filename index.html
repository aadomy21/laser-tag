How to Play

Move: WASD or Arrow Keys. Look: Mouse. Jump: Space. Shoot: Click. Reload: R. Leave: Esc twice.

On phone/tablet: left thumb for the joystick, drag right side to look, tap TAG / JUMP buttons.

You start with 6 hearts. Getting tagged costs 1 heart. Red hearts on the floor heal you (max 10).

Power Cores glow purple around the arena. Walk up and click the shrinking ring at the right moment — nail it for a bonus heart or the golden Mega Wand (6 shots instead of 5). First player to hit a core wins it.

The safe zone shrinks as the match goes on — stay near the center. Last one standing (or most hearts when time's up) gets the crown.
